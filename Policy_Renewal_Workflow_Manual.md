# Policy Renewal Workflow Manual

## Introduction

A policy renewal is the process of extending an existing insurance policy beyond its original expiration date. When a policy nears its end date, the policyholder can choose to renew it to maintain continuous coverage. This involves updating the policy dates, recalculating premiums based on current coverage, and processing payment if required.

On the app, an existing user with an active or expired policy can initiate a policy renewal. This manual provides a step-by-step guide to the policy renewal process.

---

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Active user account
- An existing policy (active or expired)
- Policy details accessible from the Policy tab

### Accessing Policy Renewal

1. Navigate to the **Policy Details** screen by selecting a policy from your policy list
2. Locate the **Renew** button at the bottom of the screen
3. Tap **Renew** to begin the renewal process

> **Note:** The Renew button is available for all policies, but renewal is particularly important for expired policies to restore coverage.

---

## Step 1: Review Policy Information

Upon entering the **Renew Policy Preference** screen, you'll see the current policy details:

- **Policy Number** - Your unique policy identifier
- **Insurance Class** - The category of insurance (e.g., Motor, Property, Health)
- **Product** - The specific insurance product type
- **Old Start Date** - Current policy's start date
- **Old End Date** - Current policy's expiration date

These details are automatically pre-filled from your existing policy and cannot be changed, as the renewal maintains the same insurance class and product type.

---

## Step 2: Set New Policy Dates

You'll need to specify the dates for your renewed policy:

### New Start Date
1. Tap on the **New Start Date** field
2. A calendar picker will appear
3. Select your desired start date (must be today or a future date)
4. The selected date will be displayed in the field

### New End Date
1. Tap on the **New End Date** field
2. A calendar picker will appear (only dates after the start date can be selected)
3. Select your desired end date
4. The selected date will be displayed in the field

### New Renewal Date
This field is **automatically calculated** as one day after your selected end date. This represents when the next renewal would be due. You don't need to enter this manually.

> **Important:** The duration between start and end dates affects the premium amount. Longer coverage periods typically result in higher premiums.

---

## Step 3: Calculate Premium

After entering the new start and end dates:

1. Tap the **Continue** button
2. The system will calculate the premium amount for your renewal based on:
   - Your policy coverage
   - The new policy duration
   - Current rates and terms
3. Wait for the calculation to complete (a loading indicator will be displayed)

Once the calculation is complete, you'll be navigated to the **Policy Summary** screen.

---

## Step 4: Review Policy Summary

The Policy Summary screen displays:

### Current Policy Information
- Policy Number
- Insurance Class
- Product
- Old Start Date
- Old End Date
- Sum Insured (coverage amount)

### New Policy Information
- New Start Date
- New End Date
- New Renewal Date
- **Premium Due** - The calculated amount for the renewal period

### Payment Information (if premium is due)
- **Charges** - Transaction fees (1.5% of premium, capped at NGN 2,000, with an additional NGN 100 for premiums above NGN 2,500)
- **Total Payment Due** - Premium plus applicable charges

Review all details carefully before proceeding.

---

## Step 5: Complete the Renewal

You have two options depending on whether payment is required:

### Option A: Payment Required (Premium Amount > NGN 0)

If a premium amount is displayed:

1. Tap the **Make Payment** button
2. You'll be directed to a secure payment gateway
3. Complete the payment using your preferred payment method
4. Upon successful payment:
   - A confirmation message will be displayed
   - Your policy will be renewed automatically
   - The new policy dates will take effect
   - You can view the updated policy in your policy list

### Option B: Submit for Approval (No Payment Required)

If the premium amount is NGN 0:

1. Tap the **Submit Quote for Approval** button
2. Your renewal request will be sent to the broker for processing
3. Wait for broker approval
4. You'll receive a notification once approved
5. Your policy will be renewed upon broker approval

> **Note:** You cannot use both options. The appropriate button will be active based on whether payment is required.

---

## Tracking Your Renewal

After submitting your renewal:

### Viewing Updated Policy
1. Navigate to the **Policy** tab
2. Find your policy in the list
3. The policy status will update based on the renewal:
   - **Active** - If the new start date has begun and payment is completed
   - **Pending** - If awaiting broker approval
   - **Expired** - If the old end date has passed but renewal is not yet complete

### Policy Details
Tap on the renewed policy to view:
- Updated policy dates
- New renewal date
- Premium amount paid
- Policy certificate (available after renewal is processed)

---

## Important Notes

### What You Can Change
- New start date
- New end date

### What You Cannot Change
- Insurance class
- Product type
- Policy number
- Sum insured
- Items insured (these remain from the original policy)

If you need to modify coverage details, consider creating a new quote or requesting a policy endorsement instead.

### Payment Gateway Charges
- Base charge: 1.5% of premium amount
- Additional charge: NGN 100 for premiums above NGN 2,500
- Maximum charge: NGN 2,000 (capped)

### Renewal Limitations
- Start date cannot be in the past
- End date must be after start date
- The same insurance class and product will be maintained
- All items insured from the original policy will be included

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem:** Premium calculation fails  
**Solution:** Check your internet connection and try again. Ensure the start and end dates are valid.

**Problem:** Payment button is disabled  
**Solution:** This is normal when no payment is required (premium = NGN 0). Use the "Submit Quote for Approval" option instead.

**Problem:** Cannot select start date  
**Solution:** Ensure you're selecting today's date or a future date. Past dates cannot be selected.

**Problem:** Renewal date field is not editable  
**Solution:** This is by design. The renewal date is automatically calculated as one day after the end date.

---

## Summary

The policy renewal process follows these key steps:

1. **Access** - Navigate to policy details and tap Renew
2. **Review** - Confirm current policy information
3. **Set Dates** - Select new start and end dates
4. **Calculate** - System calculates premium for the renewal period
5. **Review Summary** - Verify all renewal details and costs
6. **Complete** - Make payment or submit for approval
7. **Track** - Monitor your renewed policy status

Your renewed policy will provide continuous coverage based on the dates you selected, with all other terms and coverage amounts maintained from your original policy.
